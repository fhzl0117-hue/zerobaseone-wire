SEOUL — SEPTEMBER 2026

Nine became five.
The wire keeps running.

ZEROBASEONE spent the start of 2026 closing out the HERE&NOW World Tour Encore — a run that ended with back-to-back stops at K-Arena Yokohama in February and KSPO Dome in Seoul in March, capping a tour that drew more than 150,000 fans across Seoul, Bangkok, Saitama, Kuala Lumpur, Singapore, Taipei, and Hong Kong. Within days of the Seoul finale, agency WAKEONE confirmed that four members were leaving for a different label. The remaining five returned two months later with sixth mini-album Ascend-, led by title track "TOP 5." On August 16, a spoiler film teased a follow-up comeback ending on one line: "TOP 5 WILL BE BACK." Then on August 31, second Japanese EP "回帰LOVE" (Kaiki LOVE) swept the charts — #1 on Oricon's weekly album ranking, #1 daily for four straight days, #1 on Billboard Japan's Top Album Sales chart, and #1 on Tower Records' all-store album chart — backed by a two-hours'-notice guerrilla show in Tokyo. September opened with solo news: member Sung Han-bin has confirmed a solo debut for October 12, and member Kim Ji-woong has been cast to lead new multi-format drama "Siren." This page is a working build of how ZEROBASEONE Wire tracks all of it for English-speaking Zerose — translated, sourced, and converted to whatever timezone you're reading from.

A note on the lineup: ZEROBASEONE debuted in July 2023 as a nine-member group formed through the survival show Boys Planet. In March 2026, shortly after the HERE&NOW World Tour Encore concluded, agency WAKEONE confirmed that members Zhang Hao, Ricky, Kim Gyu Vin, and Han Yu Jin were departing the group; all four are reported to have signed with a different agency, YH Entertainment. WAKEONE's public comment did not detail a cause, stating only that the remaining members "are preparing to return and impress as soon as possible." ZEROBASEONE has continued as five members — Sung Han Bin, Kim Ji Woong, Seok Matthew, Kim Tae Rae, and Park Gun Wook — since. Source: Soompi.

3.7 / 5EDITOR'S STAMP
"'TOP 5' doesn't try to hide the arithmetic — it turns a smaller lineup into the whole concept, and mostly earns the swagger it's reaching for."

Ascend- (2026): ZEROBASEONE's sixth mini-album, and its first release as a five-member group. Where the group's earlier eras leaned on scale — nine voices, wide harmonies, big ensemble choreography — Ascend- is built around the opposite bet: that five members can carry a title track by sheer confidence. "TOP 5" leans into synth-driven, mid-tempo swagger rather than the group's earlier youthful pop, and the album pulls in outside collaborators to help make the turn, including producer JUNNY co-writing "Changes" and former BIGSTAR member Jvde on lyrics for "Exotic."

It's a transitional record by design — coverage around it has focused as much on the reorganization itself as on the tracks — but "TOP 5" and closer "Zero to Hundred" are the two songs making the clearest case that the smaller line-up has its own identity, not just a smaller version of the old one.
